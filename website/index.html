03what it checks

Project activity does not tell you what happens to newcomers.

Stars and issue counts describe project health. Holt looks at the path you will actually take: first-time pull requests, response times, reviews, outcomes, and the parts of the codebase where outside work has landed.

04how it works

One command. A verdict you can verify.

Holt gathers contribution history, turns it into cited findings, verifies every source, and applies a stable ruleset. A model can explain the evidence, but it cannot override the final decision.
