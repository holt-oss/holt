# Governance

Holt is maintained in public by the `holt-oss` organization. Aahil Khan is the
founding and lead maintainer.

## How decisions are made

Routine fixes and documentation changes are decided through pull-request
review. Material changes to the product scope, evidence boundary, verdict
rules, evaluation protocol, licensing, or governance begin with a public issue
so the tradeoffs are visible before implementation.

Maintainers seek rough consensus and explain the final decision when meaningful
disagreement remains. The lead maintainer is the final decision-maker while the
project has a single-maintainer team. Security and Code of Conduct matters may
be handled privately when public discussion would create additional risk.

## Project roles

- **Contributor:** anyone who participates through code, documentation,
  research, issue triage, design, or review.
- **Triager:** a trusted contributor who helps reproduce reports, organize
  issues, and guide newcomers.
- **Maintainer:** a contributor with sustained, sound work who reviews and
  merges changes and helps steward releases and project direction.
- **Lead maintainer:** responsible for access continuity, releases, governance,
  and resolving decisions that do not reach consensus.

Maintainer access is earned through repeated contributions, constructive
review, care for the project's evidence standards, and reliable participation
over time. It is not tied to employment or contribution volume alone. Existing
maintainers nominate and approve new maintainers publicly.

Inactive maintainers may step down or be moved to emeritus status after a
public check-in. Access needed for security response, package publishing, and
organization recovery should never depend permanently on one unavailable
account.

## Changes to this document

Governance changes use the same public issue and pull-request process as other
material project decisions.
